Questions

Before you book a visit

Do I really pay no brokerage?

Developers pay our fee, so the price you see is the developer's price. Ask us for the terms in writing before you book.

How do I check that a project is RERA registered?

Every home on this page shows its MahaRERA number. You can verify it yourself on the MahaRERA portal.

What happens after I book a site visit?

We confirm the slot on WhatsApp, a cab collects you from home, and an advisor shows you the project. There is no obligation to buy.

Can you help with a home loan?

We share EMI estimates and introduce you to lenders. You choose the bank and the loan terms.
